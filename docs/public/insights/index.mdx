# Insights

Insight reports are interactive reports in the OpenDataDSL portal. Each one looks at your data from a particular angle, such as what every data environment holds or how data has changed, with summary cards, tabs to drill into the detail and exports to CSV. The reports on this page are public, so they are available in every tenant and work with your own access rights.

Pick a report below to see what it shows and how to read it.

## Build your own

You can create insight reports of your own, shown in the reports menu, on a service or on a single item. See [Insights](/docs/odsl/insights/general) to get started.
