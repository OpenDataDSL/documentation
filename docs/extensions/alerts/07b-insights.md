# Insights

The Alerts screen has **Insights**: reports about your alerts that you open from the screen itself. They come with the extension (category `odsl.alerts`) and read the same alerts as the screen: yours in the data environment you are working in, and the shared alerts about the datasets you follow.

---

## The reports

| Report | What it answers |
|-|-|
| [Alert Audit Report](/docs/public/insights/alert-audit-report) | Who raised, commented on, handed over and closed your alerts, and when. Filter by period, alert, event, person and text; see the whole history of any one alert (including your team's comments on a shared alert); compare people and alerts; and see how quickly alerts were answered and closed, by priority |

![The Overview tab of the Alert Audit Report](/img/insights/alert-audit-report/overview.png)

---

## Opening a report

1. Open **Operations > Alerts**.
2. Open the screen's **Insights** and choose the report.

Each report has a **Help** link at the top that opens its own page in this documentation.

:::tip
To see everything that happened to one alert, open the **Alert Audit Report** and type the alert's id, such as `ALR-2026-000405`, in **Alert id**. You can copy the id from the alert on the Alerts screen.
:::
