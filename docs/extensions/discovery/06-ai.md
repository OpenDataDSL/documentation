# Discover AI

**Discover AI** is the first item of the **AI** menu. How Fusion AI works: chat in the Studio, assistants and the tools they use, playbooks and tasks, agents, and what it all costs.

![Discover AI](/img/extensions/discovery/ai.png)

---

## What is on the screen

### 1. Ask

Where you talk to Fusion.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Studio** | Chat with Fusion about your data | Fusion AI |
| **Assistants** | Specialists for each kind of question | Fusion AI |
| **MCP server** | Use the platform from Claude or ChatGPT | The platform |

### 2. Act

How Fusion does the work.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Tools** | What Fusion can do on the platform | Fusion AI |
| **Playbooks** | Repeatable jobs, written as documents | Fusion AI |
| **Tasks** | A playbook run, with its inputs and results | Fusion AI |

### 3. Automate

Fusion working without you.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Agents** | Questions Fusion asks by itself | Fusion AI |
| **Automations** | Run an agent when something happens | The platform |
| **AI in other screens** | Fusion helping where you work | The platform |

### 4. Track

Usage, cost and results.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Token usage and billing** | What Fusion uses, against your budget | Fusion AI |
| **Fusion insights** | Runs, results and feedback | Fusion AI |

Click any part to read about it: what it is, how it connects, key facts, where to find it and the guides to read. Parts that come with an extension your company does not have say **Not installed**.

---

## Flows

| Flow | What it shows |
|-|-|
| **Ask about your data** | What happens when you ask Fusion a question in the Studio. |
| **Run a playbook** | How a repeatable job is done the same way every time. |
| **A morning briefing agent** | How Fusion can check things for you before you start work. |
