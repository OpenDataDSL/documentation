# Discover Monitor

**Discover Monitor** is the first item of the **Monitor** menu. How the platform keeps watch: processes and their runs, dataset deliveries, curve builds, validation, alerts and the logs behind them.

![Discover Monitor](/img/extensions/discovery/monitor.png)

---

## What is on the screen

### 1. Run

What runs on the platform, and when.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Processes** | Scripts and workflows that run by themselves | The platform |
| **Executions** | Each run of a process, with its log | The platform |
| **Process timeline** | When each process ran, against when it was due | The platform |

### 2. Check

What is checked: deliveries, builds and values.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Dataset monitoring** | Did each day's data arrive, on time and complete? | The platform |
| **Curve management** | Every curve build, its status and dependencies | The platform |
| **Validation** | Checks on the values of curves and timeseries | Validation |

### 3. Raise

How problems reach the people who fix them.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Alerts** | Problems to work on, with an owner and a timeline | The platform |
| **Automations** | Tell the right people, or fix it automatically | The platform |

### 4. Investigate

Find out what happened, and spot trends.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Auditing** | Every platform log in one place | Auditing |
| **Insight reports** | Trends and summaries over time | The platform |

Click any part to read about it: what it is, how it connects, key facts, where to find it and the guides to read. Parts that come with an extension your company does not have say **Not installed**.

---

## Flows

| Flow | What it shows |
|-|-|
| **A dataset is late** | What happens when the evening settlement prices do not arrive on time. |
| **A process fails** | How a failed overnight job is spotted and investigated. |
| **Bad data is caught** | How a wrong price is stopped before it reaches a report. |
