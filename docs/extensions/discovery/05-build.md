# Discover Build

**Discover Build** is the first item of the **Build** menu. How things are built on the platform: types and calendars, scripts, notebooks and workflows, the processes that run them, extensions, and promotion between environments.

![Discover Build](/img/extensions/discovery/build.png)

---

## What is on the screen

### 1. Define

The shape of data, and how time works.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Types** | The blueprint of master data and events | The platform |
| **Calendars** | Holidays, expiries and delivery periods | The platform |

### 2. Write

The logic, in ODSL.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Scripts** | ODSL code, templates and reports | Script Manager |
| **Notebooks** | Explore data step by step | Notebooks |
| **Actions** | Reusable steps for workflows | The platform |
| **Workflows** | Actions joined into a flow | Workflow Manager |

### 3. Run

On the platform, and in the portal.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Processes** | Run a script or workflow on the platform | The platform |
| **Extensions** | Screens added to the portal | The platform |

### 4. Release

From test to production, safely.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Environments** | Production, test and preview | The platform |
| **Promotion** | Push changes between environments | Promotion |

Click any part to read about it: what it is, how it connects, key facts, where to find it and the guides to read. Parts that come with an extension your company does not have say **Not installed**.

---

## Flows

| Flow | What it shows |
|-|-|
| **From notebook to scheduled job** | How an idea tried out in a notebook becomes a job that runs every day. |
| **Design a workflow** | How a multi-step job is put together without writing it all as one script. |
| **Ship a change to production** | How a change is built and tested away from live data, then released. |
