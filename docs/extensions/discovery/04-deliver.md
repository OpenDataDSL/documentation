# Discover Deliver

**Discover Deliver** is the first item of the **Deliver** menu. How data gets onto the platform, is bundled and shaped, and is sent on to the people and systems that need it.

![Discover Deliver](/img/extensions/discovery/deliver.png)

---

## What is on the screen

### 1. Bring in

Ready-made feeds, and your own sources.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Data packages** | Install a ready-made data feed | The platform |
| **Data loaders** | Load your own files, pages and APIs | Data Loaders |

### 2. Hold and shape

The data, bundled and summarised.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Your data** | Master data, events, curves and timeseries | The platform |
| **Groups** | Bundles of items to send or export | The platform |
| **Aggregator** | Summarise any service, and report it | Aggregator |

### 3. Trigger

On a schedule, or when something changes.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Scheduled processes** | Run a job at a set time | The platform |
| **Automations** | When this happens, do that | Automations |
| **Automation targets** | What an automation does | The platform |

### 4. Send out

To people, files and downstream systems.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Export data** | Files in the layout you need | Export data |
| **Integrations** | Send data to your trading and risk systems | Integration |
| **Message queues** | Messages for your own applications | The platform |
| **APIs and add-ins** | Read the data from anywhere | The platform |

Click any part to read about it: what it is, how it connects, key facts, where to find it and the guides to read. Parts that come with an extension your company does not have say **Not installed**.

---

## Flows

| Flow | What it shows |
|-|-|
| **Load a vendor file each morning** | How a file from a data vendor becomes data your team can use, with no manual steps. |
| **Send curves to your ETRM** | How the day's curves reach your trading system after the close. |
| **A scheduled CSV export** | How a team that works in spreadsheets gets a file every evening. |
