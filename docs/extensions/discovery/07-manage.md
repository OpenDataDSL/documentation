# Discover Manage

**Discover Manage** is the first item of the **Manage** menu. How your company runs the platform: people and groups, who can see and change what, bulk changes to data, and the licences, documents and projects around it.

![Discover Manage](/img/extensions/discovery/manage.png)

---

## What is on the screen

### 1. People

Who uses the platform.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Users** | The people in your company | The platform |
| **Groups** | People, and lists of items | Group Manager |

### 2. Access

Who can see and change what.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Security policies** | Who can read and change which data | The platform |
| **Feature policies** | Which screens people see | The platform |

### 3. Data upkeep

Changing data in bulk, safely.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Master Data Management** | Change master data in bulk | Master Data Management |
| **Data Management** | Change data definitions in bulk | Data Management |

### 4. Around the data

Licences, documents and projects.

| Part | What it is | Comes with |
|-|-|-|
| **Data licences** | The licences you hold with vendors | Data Licences |
| **Documentation** | Guides and notes kept on the platform | Documentation Manager |
| **Projects** | Onboarding work, shared with OpenDataDSL | Projects |

Click any part to read about it: what it is, how it connects, key facts, where to find it and the guides to read. Parts that come with an extension your company does not have say **Not installed**.

---

## Flows

| Flow | What it shows |
|-|-|
| **Give a team access to data** | How the trading desk is allowed to update its own curves, and only those. |
| **Fix master data in bulk** | How a property is corrected on hundreds of records without editing each one. |
| **Onboard a new data feed** | How the paperwork around new data is kept in one place. |
