**Home** is the first page you see after signing in, and the top button on the rail. Instead of a menu of screens, it asks **What do you want to do today?** and shows the tasks you can do, grouped by what they are for.

![The Home page](/img/portal/layout.png)

---

## What is on the page

| Part | What it does |
|-|-|
| **Describe your task** | Type what you want to do, for example `fix the TTF curve`. The tasks are narrowed as you type. When nothing matches, select **Go** and Fusion AI suggests up to three tasks that fit. |
| **My tasks** and **All tasks** | **My tasks** are the tasks you chose for your page; **All tasks** are every task your team has. |
| **Edit my tasks** and **New task** | Choose the tasks on your page, and add tasks of your own. |
| **Group buttons** | Show one group of tasks at a time, such as **Check**, **Build** or **Deliver**; **All groups** shows them all. |
| **Task cards** | Each card says what the task does. Select it to open the screen that does it. On **All tasks**, **+** adds a task to your page and the pin removes it. |

### Needs you now
A task with open alerts moves to the top of the page under **Needs you now**, with a badge showing how many alerts are open and the most urgent one named on the card. When the alerts are closed, the task goes back to its group.

### New tasks
When extensions add tasks after you have chosen yours, a note says how many are new. Select **Show them** to see them under **All tasks**, marked **New**, or **Dismiss** to hide the note.
