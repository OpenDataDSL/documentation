The portal menu has two parts: the **rail**, a dark strip down the left with a button for each menu group, and the **panel** beside it, which lists the items in the group you select.

---

## The rail and the panel

Select a group on the rail to show its items in the panel. Each item has an icon, its name and a short description of what it is for. Select an item to open it.

![The Data group in the panel](/img/portal/menu-data.png)

- **Home** at the top of the rail opens your [Home page](/docs/user/portal/home).
- The OpenDataDSL logo opens the OpenDataDSL website.
- When you open a screen, the rail and panel move to the group that screen belongs to, so you can always see where you are.
- A group lists only what you have: an item is missing if its extension is not installed for your organisation, or you do not have access to it.

### Keeping the panel open, or the rail only
Select **Panel** at the bottom of the rail to switch between the two layouts. The portal remembers your choice.

| Layout | How it works |
|-|-|
| **Panel open** | The panel stays open beside the page. Select another group to change what it lists. Select the panel button at its top right to switch to the rail only. |
| **Rail only** | The page uses the full width. Select a group to open the panel over the page; it closes when you open an item, select the same group again, select outside it or press **Escape**. |

![The rail only, with the Data group opened over the page](/img/portal/menu-compact.png)

You can also choose the layout, and put the menu on the right instead of the left, in [Settings](/docs/user/portal/settings#environment).

## Where the old menu items went

If you used the portal before the new menu, this is where to find things now:

| You used | Now |
|-|-|
| Master Data, Data, Events, GEO Data | **Data** > **Data Explorer** (the Explore and GEO Data tabs) |
| Smart Data | **Data** > **Smart Data** |
| Process Timeline | No longer in the menu: see [Process timeline](/docs/user/portal/timeline) |
| Auditing | **Monitor** > **Auditing** |
| Groups, Favourites | **Manage** > **Group Manager**, and the starred items and groups in Data Explorer: see [Favourites](/docs/user/portal/favourites) |
| Data Packages | No longer in the menu: ask your administrator or OpenDataDSL support about a data package |
| Account, Settings | The profile menu at the top right: see [Account and settings](/docs/user/portal/settings) |
